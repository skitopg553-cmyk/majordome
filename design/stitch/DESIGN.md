## Brand & Style
This design system embodies an ultra-sleek, neo-metallic dark aesthetic marrying the precision of high-tier wealth interfaces with the technological discipline of an advanced synthetic intelligence. The visual tone is commanding, quiet, and hyper-refined.

The personality balances computational authority and executive prestige:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep obsidian ink foundations create an infinite void that reduces cognitive fatigue and prioritizes critical numerical data.
- **Architectural Metal:** Muted brushed titanium and cold steel structural planes replace arbitrary decoration, delivering an engineered tactile weight.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Restrained amethyst signals high-value actions, analytical depth, and artificial intelligence, paired with muted forest greens for balanced equilibrium and subdued night-steel blues for structural integrity.

The target demographic demands effortless mastery over high-stakes workflows: precision-focused, unhurried, and devoid of visual noise or generic neon saturation.

## Colors
The palette is calibrated strictly for deep-dark illumination, using micro-contrast and spectral discipline to isolate high-priority interactions:

- **Primary Amethyst (`#6D28D9` / `#4C1D95`):** Reserved for computational intelligence, focal financial actions, and verified states. Emits a controlled micro-glow rather than bright bloom.
- **Secondary Muted Forest (`#047857` / `#064E3B`):** Denotes physiological balance, positive yields, and operational serenity. Intentionally subdued to prevent clinical or aggressive fluorescent glare.
- **Tertiary Steel Nocturne (`#1E293B` / `#3B82F6`):** Provides cold structural scaffolding, secondary telemetry, and subtle blue-tinted metallic highlights.
- **Neutral Obsidian Planes (`#05070B`, `#080A10`, `#121620`, `#1A202C`):** Non-distracting foundation layers representing untreated obsidian and brushed aerospace titanium.

Background tiers cascade down from canvas black `#05070B` to container surfaces `#080A10` and raised interactive plates `#121620`. Text levels use `#F8FAFC` for high-emphasis data, `#94A3B8` for supportive metrics, and `#475569` for auxiliary structural metadata.

## Typography
Typographic clarity relies exclusively on `Inter` to deliver systematic, unyielding legibility across dense analytic dashboards and streamlined mobile views.

- **Numerics & Headers:** Titles and high-density telemetry utilize tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) and medium/semibold weights, preventing optical dispersion on obsidian backdrops.
- **Body & Data:** Standard reading sizes maintain neutral tracking with open aperture settings, preventing eye strain during long analytical cycles.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-indicators and uppercase labels employ expanded letter-spacing (`0.03em` to `0.06em`) with semibold weights to evoke machined aerospace hardware engravings.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Grid Dynamics:** Elements align strictly to a base 4px/8px modular cadence. Gutters scale from `1rem` on mobile to `1.5rem` on wide displays to maintain optimal scan paths.
- **Margins & Density:** Canvas margins step outward predictably (`1rem` / `2rem` / `3rem`), ensuring UI modules sit isolated from hardware bezels.
- **Section Rhythm:** Vertical padding enforces breathing room between analytic panels without fragmenting system coherence, ensuring high situational awareness at a single glance.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through metallic surface stratification, frosted obsidian glass, and precision chiseled outlines rather than diffuse, muddy drop shadows.

- **Chiseled Borders:** Surfaces employ a 1px perimeter outline composed of subtle linear gradients (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` falling off to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` or titanium `#1A202C`). This evokes diamond-cut beveled metal.
- **Backdrop Diffusion:** Modal panels and floating HUD elements use deep obsidian translucent backdrops (`rgba(8, 10, 16, 0.85)`) coupled with a heavy `16px` backdrop-filter blur.
- **Micro-Glows:** Selected interactive states or active AI directives cast a constrained ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -4px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.25)`, communicating focused power without visual glare.

## Shapes
Shapes feature disciplined, soft architecture (`roundedness: 1`), mirroring high-precision machined metals and high-tech instrument bezels:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to micro-badges, checkboxes, inputs, and segmented controls for clean geometric discipline.
- **Secondary Radius (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to telemetry cards, metric containers, interactive tiles, and modal structures.
- **Tertiary Radius (`rounded-xl: 0.75rem` / 12px):** Reserved for primary viewports, main financial cards, and structural canvas wrappers.
- **Pills & Circular Geometry:** Confined strictly to system state dots, status indicators, and full-width edge micro-switches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (AI & Core Financials):** Solid `#6D28D9` base with a subtle top edge highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` and a soft ambient glow. Active state deepens to `#4C1D95`.
- **Secondary (Titanium):** Background `#121620`, border 1px solid `#1A202C`, text `#F8FAFC`. Subtle hover shifts the background to `#1A202C` with titanium edge brightening.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, text `#94A3B8`, interactive hover state `#121620` with text `#F8FAFC`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#080A10` layered over the `#05070B` viewport. Bordered with a 1px metallic gradient outline (`#1E293B` to `#121620`).
- Padding defaults strictly to `space-md` or `space-lg`. Internal header areas feature crisp hairline dividers.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark well background `#05070B` with 1px `#1A202C` border. Text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#475569`.
- Focused state transitions border to `#6D28D9` paired with an inner edge illumination `0 0 0 1px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.4)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status (Serene):** Background `rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `rgba(4, 120, 87, 0.3)`, text `#34D399`.
- **Telemetry (Titanium):** Background `#121620`, border 1px solid `#1E293B`, text `#94A3B8`.
- Height restrained to 20px–24px with `label-sm` typography.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Checkboxes use a 16px square with 4px radius, `#080A10` background, and `#1E293B` border. Checked state transitions to `#6D28D9` with a crisp white glyph.
- Segmented switches sit inside an extruded titanium trench (`#05070B`), sliding a 1px chiseled `#1A202C` plate beneath active selections.

### AI Feedback & Data Telemetry
- Integrated analytical states emit a subtle top-border amethyst gradient illumination (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #6D28D9, transparent)`).
- Progress and volume bars use sleek 2px hairline rails in `#121620`, filled with amethyst `#6D28D9` or subdued emerald `#047857`.
